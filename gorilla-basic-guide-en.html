Basic Overlord Gorilla Guide in Last War

1. The first objective: Rookie Partner I

Many players think that simply unlocking the Gorilla is enough to use it in combat. It is not. When the Gorilla appears at the end of Season 2, it still cannot accompany your troops. To deploy it in a squad, you must reach the rank “Rookie Partner I”, and the required items are:

1,800 Training Certificates
300k Training Guidebooks
24 Bond Badges
  • If you play for free, it will take some time to deploy the Gorilla (weeks or months, still to be calculated). If you want to spend money, there are several packs you can buy to progress very quickly, and you can deploy the Gorilla in 1 week for about 500 dollars.
  • After reaching the deployment objective, you can strengthen the Gorilla with additional training, rank promotions and skill upgrades using these additional items:
Universal Overlord Shard
Overlord Skill Badge

To deploy the Gorilla, look at its base and you will see three independent training trees. You must train Attack, Defense and Survival simultaneously. The system requires all three specialties to reach certain levels to unlock the different promotions. When each specialty reaches level 100, you can promote to “Rookie Partner I” using 4 Bond Badges.

After doing so, you will be able to deploy the Gorilla in one of your squads.

At that moment, the following will also be unlocked:

  • The Gorilla’s skills.
  • Advanced promotions.
  • The complete skill tree.
